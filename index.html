Arrow Escape

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 23, 2026

Arrow Escape does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game works locally on your device and does not require an account.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information or device information. Arrow Escape does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or developer-operated servers.

Information stored on your device

The app stores only gameplay preferences and progress locally, such as:

  • your current level and completed progress;
  • your light or dark appearance choice; and
  • other settings needed for the game to function.

This information is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app. Depending on your Android backup settings, the operating system may back up app settings to your device account; we do not control or receive those backups.

Permissions

Arrow Escape does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or other sensitive device features. The current version does not require internet access.

Sharing and selling data

Because we do not collect user data, we do not sell, rent, disclose, or share user data with third parties.

Children’s privacy

Arrow Escape does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the relevant consent measures will be updated before those changes are introduced.

Security and retention

No personal data is retained on our systems. Locally stored game progress remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes or legal requirements evolve. The latest version will always be published on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions about Arrow Escape, please contact the developer through the contact details shown on the app’s Google Play Store listing.
